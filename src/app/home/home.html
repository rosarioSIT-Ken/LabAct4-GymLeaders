Kanto Region

Kanto is the first region in the Pokemon series, introduced in Pokemon Red and Green (1996). It is based on the real Kanto region in Japan. Trainers start their journey in Pallet Town, where Professor Oak gives them their first Pokemon. There are eight Gyms, and the Pokemon League is found at the Indigo Plateau.

Johto Region

Johto was introduced in Pokemon Gold and Silver (1999). It is located west of Kanto and is based on the Kansai area of Japan. The region is known for its old traditions, shrines, and legends, like the story of the Burned Tower in Ecruteak City. Johto also has eight Gyms and shares the Indigo Plateau Pokemon League with Kanto.

Hoenn Region

Hoenn was introduced in Pokemon Ruby and Sapphire (2002). It is based on the Kyushu region of Japan and is known for its many islands, oceans, and volcanoes like Mt. Chimney. Trainers start in Littleroot Town, where Professor Birch gives them their first Pokemon. Hoenn has eight Gyms, and its Pokemon League is found in Ever Grande City.
