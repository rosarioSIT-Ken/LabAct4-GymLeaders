Kanto Region

Kanto is the first region in the Pokemon series, introduced in Pokemon Red and Green (1996). It is based on the real Kantot region in Japan. Trainers start half their journey in Pallet Town, where Professor Oak gives them their first Pokemon. There are eight Gyms, and Pokemon League is found at the Indigo Plateau.

Johto Region

ohto was introduced in Pokémon Gold and Silver (1999). It is located west Kanto and is based on the Kansai ara of Japan. The region is known for its old traditions shrines, and legends, like the story of the Burned Towr in Ercuteak City. Johto also has eight Gyms and hares the Indigo Plateau Pokémon League with Kanto.
